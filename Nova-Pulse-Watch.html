Nova Pulse Projector Watch

Nova Pulse is a high-tech projector-watch concept. Rather than keeping a large display lit all the time, a button would turn on a projected wrist interface when needed and switch it off again after use. The projected controls are imagined as touch-enabled, with a companion phone providing connectivity and additional computing power.

Designed for everyday use

  • Open compatible apps, check messages, or handle calls using the projected display.
  • Access services such as TikTok, Instagram, and X when supported by a paired phone, network connection, and each app's compatibility.
  • Ask Nova, the voice-assistant concept, to open apps, place calls, or prepare message replies; the user would review or confirm actions as appropriate.
  • Track pulse and heart-rate patterns over time and offer a prompt to check in if a reading appears unusual.
  • A target of up to 72 hours per charge is part of the concept; actual battery life would depend on projection time, sensors, connectivity, and usage.

Important context

Turning off the projector when it is not needed is intended to reduce unnecessary screen use. A real product would also need accessible controls, clear consent for sharing health data, and dependable ways to notify the user if a measurement is unavailable.

These are proposed concept features, not verified medical or product specifications. Consumer heart-rate readings can be inaccurate; the watch cannot diagnose conditions or guarantee emergency-service contact. Seek medical help directly when needed and do not rely on a wearable as a substitute for care.
