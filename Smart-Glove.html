TechNova Smart Glove

The TechNova Smart Glove is a high-tech wearable concept intended to bring common phone functions onto a small display worn on the hand. It imagines combining touch input with gestures and voice so a user can choose the interaction that feels easiest for the task.

What you can do with it

  • Check notifications, preview messages, and answer or manage calls through the glove's display and controls.
  • Use voice commands for reminders, quick notes, or simple searches when speaking is convenient and appropriate.
  • View directions at a glance and control compatible smart-home devices while moving around.
  • Use configurable gestures to play or pause audio, move between screens, or trigger shortcuts.

Everyday use and design priorities

A user might pair the glove with a phone, choose which notifications are useful, then use its display or voice assistant for short tasks without taking the phone out. A real version would need a comfortable fit, controls that work for different hand movements, and a way to use essential functions if the glove battery runs low.

This is a product concept. Calls, messages, voice control, directions, and smart-home access would require compatible hardware, services, permissions, and a network connection. Users should be able to review notification and voice-data privacy settings.
