Nova's Seat

Nova's Seat is a powered mobility chair concept intended to support movement through daily routines such as travelling around home, visiting community spaces, or navigating accessible outdoor paths. The illustrated design includes supportive seating, a joystick-style control, and a tracked stair system. Actual features would depend on a finished model.

Stair climbing is a specialized capability, not a feature of ordinary powered wheelchairs. It would only be appropriate on staircases and surfaces specifically approved for that device.

How it is used

  • A mobility professional would help assess fit, posture support, control placement, and the user's needs before choosing or configuring a chair.
  • For everyday travel, the user operates the chair's controls at a comfortable speed and checks surroundings, thresholds, and ramps.
  • If a specific model supports stair mode, the user follows its instructions, secures the chair and occupant, and uses only approved stairs.
  • Charging, routine inspections, and service checks help keep a powered mobility device ready for use.

Who it aims to support

The concept is intended to prompt ideas for people who benefit from powered mobility and greater independence. Seating, controls, transfer methods, and accessibility needs vary from person to person, so a real chair must be selected with the user and qualified professionals.

This is a concept, not a tested mobility device. Do not attempt stairs with a wheelchair unless that exact model is designed and approved for stair use and the user has received proper training.
