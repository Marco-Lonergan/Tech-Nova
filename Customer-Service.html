Common product issues

Nova's Seat: the chair will not move or respond

Stop in a safe place and check the power and joystick only as described in the specific device manual. Do not force the controls or attempt stairs. If it remains unresponsive, switch it off if safe to do so and contact the device supplier or a qualified mobility technician.

Nova's Seat: battery or charging problem

Use only the charger approved for that chair and inspect the plug and cable for visible damage. Do not use damaged charging equipment or open the battery housing. If the battery is unusually hot, swollen, leaking, or smells unusual, stop charging, keep away from it, and seek qualified service.

Smart Glove: it will not pair or the display is blank

Confirm the glove and companion phone are charged, restart Bluetooth on the phone, and follow the device's pairing instructions. Keep the glove dry unless its instructions say otherwise. If it gets unusually hot or the battery looks damaged, stop using and charging it.

Nova Pulse Watch: projection or phone connection is not working

Check that projection is enabled, the surface and room lighting suit the device, and Bluetooth is on for pairing. Restart the watch and phone if needed. Pulse readings in this concept are informational only, not medical measurements; do not use them to diagnose a condition or delay medical care.

Lumen Pro: the image is blurry or causes discomfort

Pause the experience, remove the glasses, and rest your eyes. Follow the fit and focus guidance for the specific device. Do not use immersive glasses while driving or moving through hazards. Stop use if discomfort continues and seek appropriate support.

A product is overheating, damaged, or behaving unexpectedly

Stop using it. If safe, power it down and disconnect it from charging. Do not open the product or try to repair batteries yourself. Keep damaged or unusually hot devices away from people and flammable materials, and contact the product supplier or a qualified repair professional.
