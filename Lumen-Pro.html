Lumen Pro

Lumen Pro is a mixed-reality glasses concept for bringing digital content into a wearable view. In a see-through mode, the wearer could keep sight of the surroundings while viewing selected overlays. In a more immersive mode, the glasses could create a virtual environment with scenes and themes personalized to the user's preferences.

Entertainment and everyday features

  • Access compatible social media, video games, music, and other apps through a personal display.
  • Use digital overlays while keeping the outside world visible, or switch to a more immersive virtual scene.
  • Personalize virtual environments with themes and content based on interests and accessibility preferences.
  • Set a session limit that pauses the visual experience when the chosen time limit is reached.
  • Explore hands-free or simple controls so users can move between content without needing a separate handheld screen.

Comfort and responsible use

The concept includes screen-time reminders and a pause at the user's chosen limit, plus temperature monitoring and heat-management safeguards. These features would need careful engineering and testing; users should still take breaks, remain aware of their surroundings, and avoid immersive content while walking near traffic, stairs, or other hazards.

Lumen Pro is a concept, not a verified device. App access depends on compatible services and connectivity. Screen-time and temperature protections would depend on final design and testing; stop using any wearable that feels hot or uncomfortable.
