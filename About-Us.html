About TechNova

TechNova began in 2026 with a simple question: how could advanced technology help people who face barriers in everyday life? The idea grew into a collection of concepts focused on accessibility, independence, and inclusion.

TechNova explores ideas for people with disabilities and people who are marginalized or underserved. The goal is to imagine tools that could make daily activities, communication, mobility, and participation more accessible, while recognizing that everyone's needs and preferences differ.

How we approach the ideas

  • Start with everyday barriers and consider how a product might reduce friction or offer another way to complete a task.
  • Think about accessibility from the beginning, including flexible controls, clear feedback, comfort, and user choice.
  • Treat concepts as starting points for discussion and testing, not as finished or proven solutions.

Our product concepts

Nova's Seat explores powered mobility; the Smart Glove imagines phone-like access through a wearable display; Nova Pulse considers projection, voice interaction, and health trends; and Lumen Pro explores mixed-reality experiences. Each idea would need feedback from the people it aims to serve, along with engineering, safety, privacy, and usability testing before it could become a real product.

The products shown on this site are concepts and are not represented as available or clinically tested devices.
